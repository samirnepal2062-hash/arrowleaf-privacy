Arrowleaf – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Arrowleaf is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

  • Leaderboard: if you choose a display name and country, these and your score are sent to our server (Supabase) so they can appear on the public leaderboard. Do not use your real name if you do not want it shown.
  • Game progress and settings (levels, coins, theme, volume) are stored only on your device.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and usage information to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice later from Settings.

What we do not do

We do not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell personal data.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. It does not send your data anywhere.

Children

Arrowleaf is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Your choices

You can reset ad consent from Settings (where shown), reset your advertising ID in Android settings, or ask us to remove your leaderboard entry by writing to the address below.
